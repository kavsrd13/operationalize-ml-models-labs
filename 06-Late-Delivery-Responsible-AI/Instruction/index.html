Exercise 06: Late delivery and Responsible AI

Goal

Train a late-delivery classifier, register its model, and inspect cohort errors, feature explanations, and the Responsible AI dashboard.

Steps

  1. Complete exercise 00. In PowerShell at the collection root set $RG and $WS to your shared resource group and workspace. Confirm olist-course-cpu exists.
  2. Read ../prepare.py: identify the label, checkout-time features, full train/test split, and smaller stratified dashboard copies.
  3. Run the preparation script with the Olist environment:
   .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe .\06-Late-Delivery-Responsible-AI\prepare.py
  1. Inspect the Parquet and MLTable files under ../data. Register the four named assets in the same workspace:
   Get-ChildItem .\06-Late-Delivery-Responsible-AI\data\*.yml | ForEach-Object {
     az ml data create -f $_.FullName -g $RG -w $WS
   }
  1. Submit and monitor the training job:
   az ml job create -f .\06-Late-Delivery-Responsible-AI\jobs\train.yml -g $RG -w $WS
   az ml job stream -n YOUR_TRAIN_JOB -g $RG -w $WS
  1. In Studio inspect the full future-period classification metrics. When the job is Completed, register its MLflow output:
   az ml model create -n olist-late-delivery-rai --type mlflow_model `
     --path 'azureml://jobs/YOUR_TRAIN_JOB/outputs/artifacts/paths/outputs/model/' `
     -g $RG -w $WS
  1. Check the returned model version. ../jobs/rai-dashboard.yml references version 1; if yours differs, change its selected_model.path and construct.inputs.model_info to the version you just registered.
  2. Read ../lab-guide.html for the dashboard's component flow, then submit:
   az ml job create -f .\06-Late-Delivery-Responsible-AI\jobs\rai-dashboard.yml -g $RG -w $WS
   az ml job stream -n YOUR_DASHBOARD_JOB -g $RG -w $WS
  1. Open the dashboard in Studio. Compare error and explanation views across cohorts. Observed differences alone do not establish causality or a fairness violation.
